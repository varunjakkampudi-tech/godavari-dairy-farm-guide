# Blockers and external prerequisites

These items cannot be honestly completed from general internet research alone. Keep the software useful while they remain open; do not invent values or professional approvals.

| ID | Needed evidence / decision | Why external | Safe way to close | Affected checklist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXT-01 | Current milk acceptance, testing, unit, payment and deduction terms from at least two nearby buyers, with dated settlement examples | Buyer access and terms vary by collection route and can change | Obtain written terms and anonymized dated slips with permission; confirm new-supplier eligibility | Q03, C17, C18, DEC01 |
| EXT-02 | Attributable farmer interviews and local farm case studies | Interviews and visits require real people, access and consent | Conduct consent-based interviews; label self-report vs documents vs observation | Q04, D17 |
| EXT-03 | Lease, boundaries, flood/waterlogging history, water, power, road, drainage outfall and permissions for the actual parcel | Site conditions cannot be inferred from “one acre” | Survey and inspect the actual plot, including after rain; obtain written lease rights | Q03, L01-L04 |
| EXT-04 | Animal availability, identity, observed multi-day yield, clinical exam, disease tests and transport/quarantine plan | Individual animals require physical inspection and veterinary judgment | Arrange independent local veterinarian and witnessed milkings before purchase | A01-A04, DEC01 |
| EXT-05 | Current local fodder/feed supply, composition, crop suitability, irrigation and delivered prices | Crop, season, soil, water and market conditions vary locally | Request dated Agriculture/Animal Husbandry/University advice and vendor quotes | F01-F04 |
| EXT-06 | Clinical review of disease, vaccine, medication, reproduction and emergency content | Requires a qualified veterinarian and current local program knowledge | Have a licensed local veterinarian review named pages; record date, scope and qualifications | H01-H04, V01-V02, R15, EM01 |
| EXT-07 | Plot-specific dimensions, drainage, flood level, structure, electrical/water and cost design | Requires survey and competent civil/agricultural engineering | Obtain measured site plan and engineer review; identify what is temporary on leased land | L01-L04 |
| EXT-08 | Telugu terminology and translation review | Machine translation is not native-speaker validation | Translate with a competent Telugu editor and obtain dairy/veterinary terminology review | E02, I01-I02 |
| EXT-09 | Current legal applicability for the owner's sales route, lease, construction, labor, tax, transport and insurance | Rules depend on entity, route, parcel and current local enforcement | Confirm with FSSAI/FoSCoS, district/municipal authorities and a qualified legal/accounting adviser | LEG01-02 |
| EXT-10 | Verified on-call veterinary contacts/hours for Bhimavaram, Tanuku, Palakollu and nearby villages | Public directory entries are not emergency service confirmation | Call official district/clinic numbers; record date, coverage and whether it is truly on-call | D16, EM02 |
