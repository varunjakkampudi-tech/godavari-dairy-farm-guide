## Major gaps

- The health, breed, feed, legal, seasonal and milking content is introductory rather than the requested complete handbook; professional review is absent.
- Breed registry filter and purchase comparison exist, but full breed profiles/photos and a nutrient-calculating ration planner are absent. The symptom navigator is a first-response routing aid, not a complete disease knowledge base or clinically reviewed decision tool. Local buyer/supplier directory, verified farmer case study and phone-confirmed emergency service directory remain absent.
- The finance studio is not a fully validated financial product: no price-by-fat/SNF integration into its 365-day model, detailed amortization, validated animal lactation curves, complete multi-year projections, verified local costs or accountant review. Several cost categories remain approximate or manually grouped.
- Records use a generic event ledger for many categories. Module-specific reporting/forms, additional quota/data-loss tests and professional review remain.
- Telugu localization is partial: shared navigation and basic controls have a persisted language switch, but substantive guide content remains English and the strings need native-speaker review. The browser suite now covers 14 functional/accessibility/offline/mobile checks locally; employee workflows now include a guide, repeatable checklists and relief plan, but not a complete role-based mode. Broader accessibility/security checks and full requirement coverage remain absent. A real mobile overflow was found and fixed during this browser pass. The PWA install flow was only synthetically prompted; actual installation and mobile behavior remain unverified.
