Local evidence already confirmed

  • ICAR-CIRB identifies a Godavari buffalo tract that includes Tanuku, Bhimavaram and Narasapuram; this supports including local animals in breed comparisons, not assuming any seller’s individual animal is purebred or productive.
  • West Godavari District lists its Animal Husbandry office in Gunupudi, Bhimavaram, and a State Institute of Animal Health referral facility at Tanuku; call ahead to verify hours and coverage.
  • FSSAI’s March 2026 advisory makes registration relevant for producers outside the specified cooperative arrangement and milk vendors; verify the actual category before selling.
