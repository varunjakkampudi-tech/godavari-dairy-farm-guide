Farm records

Keep animal, milk, expense and daily operation records on this device. Use a farm-specific ID for every animal and export a backup regularly.

Privacy and backup: Entries are stored only in this browser’s local storage. They are not encrypted or synced, and can be erased by clearing browser data or losing the device. Export JSON/CSV after updates. Do not store highly sensitive personal, banking or identity data.

Editing updates the selected record. Deletion asks for confirmation. All records remain on this device.

Backup and transfer

Version-1 records are copied to the new register on this browser. The original version-1 storage key is retained as a recovery copy until you choose to clear all records. JSON import accepts version-1 and version-2 backups; imports replace the current local data only after confirmation.
