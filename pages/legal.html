Legal, lease and compliance checklist

Rules depend on the parcel, land classification, construction, water source, exact sale arrangement and business turnover. This page flags questions for the relevant authority; it is not legal advice.

Evidence and safety: General guidance only. Confirm plot, buyer, legal and veterinary decisions with current local authorities and qualified professionals. Unverified regional details remain explicitly marked.

Why it matters

Lease: have a local property lawyer or revenue adviser check ownership, authority to lease, permitted agricultural/livestock activity, term, registration/stamp requirements, access, water, power, building consent, manure and wastewater responsibility, notice/renewal, insurance, improvement ownership and restoration. Do not assume a verbal lease permits construction.

Practical guidance

Milk: FSSAI’s 11 March 2026 advisory says milk producers must obtain the applicable registration/licence unless both conditions apply: they are registered members of a dairy cooperative society and sell/supply all their milk to that society. Milk vendors are also covered by the advisory. If either cooperative condition is not met, confirm the applicable requirement and exact FoSCoS category with FSSAI before starting sales; processing or retailing may involve additional requirements.

Use this safely

Ask Gram Panchayat/ULB and relevant departments about shed/building permission, land use, drainage/wastewater, water extraction and electricity connection. Confirm animal transport and identification requirements with the veterinarian/transport authority.

Local application

Schemes, insurance subsidy and loan support change. Seek a dated written eligibility answer from the district office, insurer or bank; do not include a scheme in the base-case budget until approved.
