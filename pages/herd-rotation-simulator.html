How rotation is modeled

Adults are distributed evenly through a repeated calving interval at the starting point. For each simulated day, the model treats an animal as in milk when its modeled days since calving are less than the entered lactation length. A flat milk yield is used while in milk; real yield declines and varies by stage. No pregnancy confirmations, sale of calves, replacement purchases, animal deaths, loan cash flows or startup capital costs are forecast automatically. The herd is assumed fully purchased and in place on Day 1; this is a steady-state capacity model, not the 4+2+2 staged buying cash flow. For the first six months of the actual launch, compare a separate staged-purchase budget and enter only the adults truly present.

Buyer settlement modeling: daily earned milk receipts are delayed by the number of days entered; cash reserve is reduced by daily feed and one-thirtieth of fixed/extra monthly costs. All simulated months are 30 days; this is a planning convention, not a calendar prediction.

Monthly animal-health plan: use real dates in the breeding calendar and veterinarian-confirmed expected calving windows. Recompute each month as actual service, pregnancy, dry-off and milk logs replace assumptions. For phased purchase order see 8-animal rotation plan. For financing and depreciation see finance studio.
