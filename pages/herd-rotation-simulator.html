Milk continuity, calf growth and real cash runway

This phased-purchase scenario starts with 4 adults, adds 2 at month 4 and 2 at month 7. It models animal-specific production stages, variable milk output, calving, calf holding costs, settlement delays and liquid cash over 24 months.

Critical: No dairy investment guarantees zero-loss days. The model's biological timing and yield curve are illustrative, not veterinary predictions. ₹75/L is an unverified buyer price. A buffalo observed producing 10+ L/day does not necessarily average 10 L throughout lactation. Do not purchase until the conservative scenario passes finance, buyer, feed, health and reserve gates.

How to interpret and update the forecast

The model uses a normalized, illustrative declining lactation curve. Each animal's reproductive stage is assumed to repeat at the entered interval; calving dates, conception, heifer sex and health are not guaranteed. It counts costs for calves born inside the modeled horizon, with zero calf sale revenue by default, because no local sale contract exists. It cannot decide whether a calf should be kept or sold. Calf revenue is excluded from the conservative financial case, and vet costs are assumptions. Milk-buyer settlement is paid after the chosen delay.

It excludes depreciation, taxes, land-sale value, value of unsold animals and actual resale valuation. To assess total economic profit, independently compute asset depreciation, opportunity cost of capital, loan interest, and owner labor; enter cash payments in the other/loan fields where appropriate. The default setup is not a verified local quote and the balance is cash remaining from initial investment, not accounting profit. For actual dates, use the animal breeding calendar.
