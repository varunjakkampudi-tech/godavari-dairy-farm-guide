Hygienic milking standard procedure

Prepare a clean, dry milking location and clean food-grade containers. Keep sick, treated or abnormal milk separate according to veterinarian and buyer instructions. Wash hands and inspect the buffalo before milking.

Evidence and safety: General guidance only. Confirm plot, buyer, legal and veterinary decisions with current local authorities and qualified professionals. Unverified regional details remain explicitly marked.

Why it matters

Clean visibly soiled teats with safe clean water or an approved method, then dry each teat with an individual clean disposable towel or designated clean towel. Check foremilk into a strip cup or appropriate surface for clots, flakes, watery appearance or blood. Do not mix abnormal milk with sale milk.

Practical guidance

Milk with clean hands or sanitized machine equipment, consistently and gently. Avoid wet-hand milking, pulling, floor contamination or using a shared dirty cloth. Apply a suitable post-milking teat disinfectant according to its label and veterinary advice.

Use this safely

Filter using a clean food-grade filter; measure and record milk by animal and milking; protect from dust, insects and heat. Deliver promptly to the buyer’s validated collection/cooling process. If chilling is used, follow applicable dairy guidance and equipment instructions; no universal safe delay is claimed here.

Local application

After milking, disassemble and clean equipment using manufacturer-recommended detergent, water temperature and sanitation steps. Dry and store protected. Follow veterinary treatment withdrawal periods and the buyer’s rejection rules exactly.
