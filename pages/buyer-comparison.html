Check before relying on the result

  1. Confirm the buyer accepts buffalo milk from a new supplier and obtain the current written price method.
  2. Confirm whether the rate is per litre, per kg milk, or per kg fat. Do not convert without the buyer’s stated or measured density.
  3. Check fat/SNF test method, sample handling, calibration, rejection rules, deductions, transport costs and payment delay.
  4. Compare at least two actual settlement slips over multiple weeks. A single quotation does not establish future price or access.
