Milk buyers and payment terms

A retail packet price is not a farm-gate price. Buffalo milk may be priced by fat and/or SNF, by litre/kg, quality grade, or a buyer-specific formula, with deductions, transport costs, membership rules and payment delays. Never translate a butterfat rate directly into ₹/litre without the buyer’s formula and lab result.

Evidence and safety: General guidance only. Confirm plot, buyer, legal and veterinary decisions with current local authorities and qualified professionals. Unverified regional details remain explicitly marked.

Why it matters

Before livestock purchase, ask the collection center: Do you accept new buffalo suppliers? What are hours and minimum quantity? How is fat/SNF tested and calibrated? Can the farmer witness the test? What are rejection thresholds and antibiotic/adulteration checks? What is the rate chart and how often does it change? What deductions, membership fees, loan recovery or transport apply? When is settlement paid?

Practical guidance

Request at least 8–12 recent anonymized settlement slips from a current supplier, including liters/kg, fat, SNF, rate, deductions and payment date. Ask about seasonal limits and cooling/collection responsibilities. Do not publish a center as a verified buyer until access is confirmed directly.

Use this safely

Direct sale may earn a higher gross price but adds delivery, customer acquisition, containers, chilling, spoilage, compliance and time. Compare net contribution and workload rather than headline price. FSSAI registration/licensing obligations depend on the actual sales arrangement.
