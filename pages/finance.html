What the calculation means

Gross milk revenue = herd × lactating days × average lactating yield × saleable fraction × price. Annual cash operating costs = monthly costs × 12 plus annual costs. Operating margin = milk and other income minus listed operating costs. It excludes owner labor, financing, tax, depreciation, replacement stock, mortality, manure/by-product value, and capital recovery unless you include them in “other.”

The model does not predict how animals’ lactations overlap. Enter annual average lactating days from an individual animal calendar; a single average can hide a cash-flow shortage. Add a worst-case scenario with fewer milking days, lower saleable yield/price and higher feed costs.
