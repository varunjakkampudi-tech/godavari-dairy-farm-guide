Is ₹70–₹80 per litre a confirmed buffalo-milk buying price?

No. It is a target assumption, not a verified local procurement offer. Ask the actual buyer for current written rates, the flat/fat/SNF formula, quality rules, deductions, rejection conditions and payment dates. Compare the buyer settlement to your measured milk and costs. Milk market guide · Buyer comparison tool.

Can this guide tell me whether my farm will be profitable?

Not without your real buyer terms, animal records, feed and labor costs, lease/site costs, veterinary budget and working capital. The finance studio calculates entered assumptions; its results are not a forecast or investment recommendation. Record dated evidence and review it with an accountant or qualified adviser. Finance studio.

Can one acre safely fit four buffaloes and all farm activities?

An acre alone cannot establish suitability. Confirm usable area, access, drainage/flood exposure, water, wastewater route, lease permissions, setbacks and local construction rules on the actual parcel. The site diagram is conceptual, not surveyed or engineer-approved. Farm layout and site checklist.

Which buffalo breed should a beginner buy?

The guide does not guarantee yield or rank breeds. Compare locally available Godavari-type and registered-breed animals using identity and records, observed milkings across days, buyer acceptance, health examination and delivered cost. A regional type label does not prove an individual animal’s ancestry or production. Breed library · Purchase workflow.

How do I check an animal’s milk yield before purchase?

Ask permission to observe morning and evening milking on multiple days, record the amount and conditions, compare with dated records, and have an independent veterinarian examine the animal. One claimed or witnessed milking is not a full production history. Purchase checklist.

How much feed should I give each buffalo?

This app does not prescribe a ration. Requirements depend on body weight, production, pregnancy/lactation, forage quality and other conditions. Measure available feed and discuss a balanced ration with a qualified nutrition professional; do not treat the fodder-area estimate as a diet. Feed and fodder planning.

Can the health pages diagnose an illness or tell me which medicine to use?

No. They help identify warning signs and prepare information for a veterinarian. They do not diagnose, prescribe, calculate doses or replace an examination. Contact a veterinarian promptly when an animal is unwell; urgent signs require immediate help. Health guide and symptom triage.

What should I do if a buffalo has breathing difficulty, collapses or has severe bloat?

Keep people safe, avoid forcing anything into the animal’s mouth or attempting an untrained procedure, and contact a veterinarian immediately. Share the animal’s ID, signs and when they began. The listed district office number is not a verified 24-hour hotline. Emergency symptom guidance · Published veterinary contacts and limits.

Does the guide give a fixed vaccination schedule?

No. Disease risk, current campaigns and each animal’s situation require confirmation by the local government veterinarian. Ask about eligibility, timing, pregnancy considerations, records and follow-up; do not administer vaccine based only on a generic web calendar. Vaccination planning.

Can one hired worker cover every task all year?

One person needs rest, leave and emergency backup. Set a realistic schedule with the buyer’s pickup time, named relief contacts, safe task boundaries and owner handover. The illustrative planner is not a staffing guarantee or legal work-hours opinion. Daily schedule · Launch and employee workbook.

Is the whole website available in Telugu?

No. The interface switch covers selected navigation and controls, while most guide content remains in English. Telugu search keywords map to English search records for discovery; they are not translations, and native-speaker review has not occurred.

Are farm records backed up to the cloud?

No. Animal and farm records are stored in this browser on this device. They are not encrypted, synchronized or automatically backed up. Export and keep an independent copy, and avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices. Farm records and backups.

Has a local farmer, buyer or animal seller verified the figures in this app?

No. No farm interviews, buyer calls, site surveys, seller checks or animal inspections have been conducted for this project. The guide marks these as evidence gaps rather than inventing quotes or success stories. Local fieldwork plan · Sources and evidence limits.
