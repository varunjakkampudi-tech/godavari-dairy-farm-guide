Local farms and field interviews

No direct farm visits, calls, or owner interviews have yet been conducted. The website does not invent successful farms, farmer reviews, profits, milk prices, suppliers or quotations.

Evidence and safety: General guidance only. Confirm plot, buyer, legal and veterinary decisions with current local authorities and qualified professionals. Unverified regional details remain explicitly marked.

Why it matters

Interview protocol: request consent; record date, village/mandal, herd size, breed evidence, stage/lactation records, feed source, water, labor, buyer/payment method, health costs, capital and operating costs, dry periods, losses, and challenges. Ask what is self-reported and request anonymized supporting records with permission.

Practical guidance

Cross-check claims with more than one source. Separate observed facts, documents, recall estimates and opinion. Do not publish names, phone numbers, photos, faces, geolocation or financial records without explicit permission.

Use this safely

Compare Bhimavaram, Tanuku, Palakollu and nearby villages using the same worksheet: buyer access and written terms; travel time; veterinary response; lease and permitted shed; dry-season water; flood/drainage exposure; fodder/feed availability; labor and backup; and construction/transport quotes.
