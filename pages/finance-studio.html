Model limits

The calendar estimates each animal at a constant entered yield while lactating, with a fixed lactation/dry cycle in a non-leap 365-day calendar with actual month lengths and a user-entered saleable fraction. It does not model a biologically validated lactation curve, pregnancy diagnosis, variable feed intake, seasonality by district, mortality probability, tax law, or actual loan terms. Buyer fat/SNF rates and tested monthly composition must be obtained locally. Age and lactation-stage labels are planning notes; entered yield, days in milk and calving month drive production. Enter illness yield loss as a stress case. Depreciation is shown in the operating result but is non-cash; EMI affects cash flow. Payback is omitted when annual net cash is zero or negative. Compare several scenarios and consult a local accountant, veterinarian and buyer before committing funds.
