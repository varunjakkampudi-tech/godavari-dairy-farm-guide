Evidence types: what a source can and cannot establish

The source register assigns a curated source class. That describes the kind of publication, not its accuracy, quality, current status, or whether a specific sentence is supported. Read every source’s scope and limitations.

Evidence typeHow to interpret itCurrent guide boundary
Official rule or programme guidancePublished government direction; check jurisdiction, current version and adoption.National guidance does not establish the current Andhra Pradesh campaign or an individual animal’s eligibility.
Technical guidance / research profileEducational or research material; population data are not individual performance.Buffalo health, feed and breed content is general; qualified review is still required.
Historical statistic or observationKeep its date and geography attached; do not present it as current.Historical district, breed or weather data do not prove today’s local farm conditions.
Administrative directoryA published listing does not confirm current hours, response or service availability.Listed office contacts are not verified emergency/on-call coverage.
Farmer or seller reportAttribute speaker and date; separate memory, documents and direct observation.No local farmer interview or seller performance claim is collected in this guide.
Model assumption / calculated outputUser-entered scenario and its arithmetic, not an observation, forecast or recommendation.Calculator outputs depend on user inputs; the ₹70–₹80/L target is not a verified buyer rate.
Unverified local claimState what is unknown and the evidence task needed before relying on it.Buyer terms, plot risk, local yields, quotes and farm profitability remain unverified.
Professional judgment / physical inspectionRecord the qualified reviewer, date and scope; inspection requires seeing/measuring the actual subject.No veterinary approval, animal inspection, plot survey or engineering sign-off is claimed.
Market contextConsumer prices and national context are not producer payment evidence.Do not enter consumer milk prices as farm-gate revenue.
Established factA narrow claim checked against a current authoritative source passage that directly supports that sentence.An official publisher alone does not make every nearby claim established.
Research findingKeep the study population, method, date and limitations attached to its result.Breed/population averages do not predict an individual animal.
Historical statistic or observationKeep the reporting period and geography; do not imply it is current.The West Godavari planning and climate references are historical context, not current site evidence.
