## Required field verification before a business recommendation

- **Milk buyer:** Identify 2–3 centres near the candidate plots; ask for current buffalo milk intake and payment formula, fat/SNF testing and calibration, collection schedule, rejection rules, deductions, payment lag, chilling needs and written farmer settlement examples covering multiple weeks.
- **Farm economics:** Obtain dated local quotations for lease, construction, water and pump, electricity, fencing, feed, fodder seed/planting, transport, equipment, labour, veterinary services and insurance. Confirm whether costs include delivery, taxes and maintenance.
- **Livestock:** Ask the local government veterinarian for current disease/vaccination and quarantine protocols. Require animal identity, actual yield checks across multiple milkings, health/reproduction records and independent pre-purchase examination.
- **Candidate site:** Record plot boundaries/dimensions, road access, seasonal waterlogging/flood history, water quality/availability, drainage outlet, power, neighbour setbacks and written lessor permission for livestock and construction.
- **Location comparison:** Evaluate Bhimavaram, Tanuku, Palakollu and nearby villages against actual buyer access, veterinary response, fodder/feed availability, lease price, water, flood exposure and labour. Do not rank locations without comparable local data.
- **Case studies:** Do not claim a local farm is successful or profitable without a named attributable source and clear evidence type. Conduct interviews only with permission and document whether figures are self-reported.
