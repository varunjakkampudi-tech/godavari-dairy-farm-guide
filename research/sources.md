# Research sources

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Online availability and facts may change; recheck before acting. The source list distinguishes official guidance from contextual or historical data.

## Official and technical sources

| Topic | Source | Use and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Buffalo health and disease education | [ICAR-CIRB Buffalopedia: Health Management](https://buffalopedianew.cirb.res.in/health-management-2/) | Reference material for signs and buffalo health topics; not permission for untrained treatment. Verify vaccine and medicine details with current official advice. |
| Clinical mastitis | [NDDB: Clinical Mastitis](https://www.nddb.coop/farmer/animal-health/disease/mastitis/clinical) | Signs include udder and milk changes and possible fever; prevention includes clean milk production. Diagnosis and treatment require a veterinarian. |
| Heat stress | [NDDB: Managing Summer Stress in Dairy Animals](https://www.nddb.coop/node/1376) | General heat management context; implement for actual weather, facility and animal condition. |
| Buffalo housing/heat | [ICAR-CIRB Buffalopedia: Shelter Management](https://buffalopedianew.cirb.res.in/shelter-management/) | Explains buffalo heat sensitivity and shelter/cooling considerations; site design remains plot-specific. |
| Milk handling | [NDDB: Milk processing and storage reference](https://www.nddb.coop/ccnddb/milkipedia) | Raw milk handling/chilling and equipment hygiene context; actual handling must meet applicable requirements and buyer process. |
| Animal disease control | [DAHD: National Animal Disease Control Programme](https://dahd.gov.in/en/schemes/programmes/nadcp) | Programme overview for FMD and brucellosis; programme objectives are not a substitute for a current local vaccination calendar. |
| Buffalo husbandry and housing | [ICAR-CIRB Buffalopedia: Shelter Management](https://buffalopedianew.cirb.res.in/shelter-management/) | Technical reference for shelter and management. Apply with local veterinary/agricultural advice and site conditions. |
| Godavari buffalo | [ICAR-CIRB Buffalopedia: Godavari](https://buffalopedianew.cirb.res.in/godavari/) | Identifies a breeding tract in East and West Godavari including Tanuku, Bhimavaram and Narasapuram. Historical average production/reproductive traits are reference-population data, not guarantees for a purchased animal. |
| Official breed registry | [ICAR-NBAGR: Buffalo breeds of India](https://nbagr.res.in/node/114) | Official registered-breed list and home tracts. Consult current list; a local seller's breed label still needs verification. |
| Buffalo nutrition | [NDDB: Feeding of Dairy Animals](https://nddb.coop/farmer/animal-nutrition/feeding-of-dairy-animals) | General feeding principles; not an individualized ration. |
| Ration balancing | [NDDB: Ration Balancing Programme](https://nddb.coop/services/animalnutrition/programmes/ration-balancing-programme) | Supports animal-specific balancing based on type, age, yield, fat and feed regime. |
| Buffalo compound feed | [NDDB: Compound Cattle Feed](https://nddb.coop/services/animalnutrition/cattlefeed) | Includes indicative feeding examples; verify actual feed label and animal requirements. |
| Breed/census sources | [DAHD: Livestock census and statistics](https://dahd.gov.in/en/schemes/programmes/animal-husbandry-statistics) | Official census and breed-report entry point; check report edition and whether draft/final before citing. |
| National buffalo breed reference | [DAHD: 20th Livestock Census instruction manual](https://www.dahd.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-07/20thLscInstructionManual.pdf) | Contains registered breed and tract material; older edition, use newer official breed reports when available. |
| West Godavari dairy context | [NABARD: West Godavari PLP 2022–23](https://www.nabard.org/auth/writereaddata/tender/0501223757west-godavari.pdf) | Historical district context; contains 2018–19 production and 2019 livestock-census figures and named collectors. Not proof of current procurement terms or operational buyer access. |
| Local veterinary services | [West Godavari District Animal Husbandry Department](https://westgodavari.ap.gov.in/departments/ah/) | Official district page lists the district AH office in Gunupudi, Bhimavaram, and the State Institute of Animal Health at Tanuku as a referral facility. Verify current hours, contacts and service coverage directly. |
| Food safety registration | [FSSAI advisory dated 11 March 2026 for milk producers and vendors](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Advisory%20dated%2011_03_2026%20related%20to%20Milk%20Producers%20and%20Milk%20vendors.pdf) | Says non-cooperative-member producers and vendors must obtain applicable registration/licence; it describes an exception for registered cooperative members who sell/supply all milk to that society. Confirm business-specific category with FSSAI/FoSCoS. |
| Consumer price context | [NCDFI Civil Consumer Prices](https://www.ncdfi.coop/index.php/civil-consumer-prices/) | Consumer prices, not producer procurement prices; never use as farm revenue. |

## Research rules

- Record access date and the exact claim supported in the verification log.
- Prefer current government, university and peer-reviewed evidence for health, legal and scheme claims.
- Treat farmer-reported claims and media reports as attributed reports, not verified farm accounts.
- Do not publish local prices, sellers, veterinary contacts or scheme availability without direct current verification.
- For veterinary content, provide escalation and prevention education; do not give layperson medication doses or replace diagnosis.
