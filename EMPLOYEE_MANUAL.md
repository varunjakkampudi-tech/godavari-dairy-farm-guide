# Employee field manual

This is an interim guide for training design, not a veterinarian-approved employee procedure. Owner and qualified local veterinarian must train staff for the actual animals, equipment, buyer rules and site.

## Each shift

- Approach calmly; observe each animal before handling. Note appetite, water, breathing, posture, walking, dung, udder/milk and unusual behavior.
- Follow the farm's written animal-specific ration. Do not change medicines or feed abruptly without the owner/veterinarian's instruction.
- Keep milking hands, teats, towels, containers and equipment clean. Keep abnormal or treatment-withheld milk separate and follow the buyer/vet direction.
- Measure and record milk by animal/session. Report a sudden drop or abnormal milk promptly.
- Refill/check clean water; remove manure safely; keep floors non-slip and drains clear; protect stored feed from rain, mold and pests.
- Before handover, state animal concerns, low supplies, equipment faults, milk delivered/rejected, tasks not done and who was contacted.

## Call the owner and veterinarian urgently

Breathing distress, collapse, inability to stand, severe bloat, suspected poisoning, difficult calving, heavy bleeding, severe dehydration, rapidly spreading illness, or dangerous heat/flood conditions require urgent escalation. Do not inject, drench, pull a calf, or give leftover medicine unless a veterinarian directly instructs a trained person.

## Checklist status

Ticking a browser checklist only records a local action; it is not independent verification or secure attendance. Do not use a shared device for private notes. A worker needs clear shift/rest arrangements, safe equipment, training and relief coverage.
